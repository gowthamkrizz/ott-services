Support FAQ

Quick answers to common support questions

Go to the Login page and click "Forgot password?". Enter your registered email and we'll send you a password reset link within seconds. The link is valid for 24 hours.

Log in to your Dashboard → Subscription → Cancel Plan. You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees ever.

Use the contact form above with "Technical Issue" or "Content Request" as the subject. Include as much detail as possible — device type, browser, the title in question and steps to reproduce the issue.
