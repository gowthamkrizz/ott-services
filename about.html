🎬 OUR STORY

Born from a Passion for Storytelling

Stackly was founded in 2020 by a team of tech innovators and entertainment veterans who believed every person deserves access to world-class stories — without the bloated cable bills and terrible user experiences.

We started with a simple vision: build a streaming platform that respects viewers — no ads, no dark patterns, just brilliant content delivered beautifully. Today, with over 50 million subscribers across 190+ countries, that vision has become a global movement.

From our roots in Mumbai to a team of 2,000+ spanning 15 countries, Stackly continues to push the boundaries of what streaming can be.

Our Journey

From a bold idea to a global platform — the Stackly story

2020

The Beginning

Stackly founded in Mumbai with a team of 12. Launched beta with 500 titles and 10,000 early-access users.

2021

Series & 1M Subscribers

Expanded to 1 million subscribers. Launched original series division and secured content deals across 20 studios.

2022

Global Expansion

Expanded to 50+ countries. Introduced 4K streaming and the Stackly Originals label. 10M subscribers milestone.

2023

Award Season

Stackly Originals won 14 international awards. Surpassed 30M subscribers. Launched offline download feature.

2024

50M & Growing

Reached 50 million subscribers across 190+ countries. Launched Dolby Atmos audio, AI recommendations and live sports.
